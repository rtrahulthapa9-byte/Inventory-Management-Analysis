# 📦 Inventory Management MIS Report

> 📌 **Note:** This is a dummy project created for learning and practice. All data used in this project is sample data.

 **Reporting Period:** January – June 2024                           
 **Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel
 


## About the Project

This project is an **Inventory Management MIS (Management Information System) Report** that analyzes a store's inventory and sales performance over 6 months.

The report helps understand stock movement, sales trends, product performance, refill requirements, and delivery performance.

### What the Report Includes

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
|  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) | Total sales value, stock value, units sold, sell-through rate, low stock products, urgent requirements, and on-time delivery % |
|  Monthly Performance | Month-on-month revenue and units sold |
|  Category Performance | Units sold, reorder counts, stock value, and sales value across 9 categories |
|  Product Refill Requirements | Urgent and upcoming refill requirements for each product |
|  Top 10 Urgent Products | Products that need immediate restocking |
|  Delivery Performance | Average delivery days by category |


## Problem Statement

The business holds a large amount of inventory, but it is not always clear:

- How much stock is selling and how much remains unsold?
- Which products are running out while others remain in stock?
- Which categories are performing well or poorly?
- Are sales increasing or decreasing over time?
- Are suppliers delivering stock on time?

Without proper reporting, it becomes difficult to decide **what to purchase, how much to purchase, and when to reorder**.

This can lead to blocked capital, excess inventory, stock shortages, and lost sales.


## Project Objectives

1. Track sales and stock performance in one report.
2. Identify the best and weakest performing categories.
3. Find products that require urgent restocking.
4. Monitor supplier delivery performance.
5. Support better inventory and purchasing decisions.


## 🔍 4. Key Insights

1. Out of 52.73M in stock value, only 26.34M was converted into sales, indicating a large amount of capital tied up in inventory.

2. The stock sell-through rate is 33.3%, showing that inventory is moving slowly.

3. Monthly revenue remained between 4.27M and 4.48M, with June recording the lowest revenue.

4. Beverages recorded the highest sales volume with 60,831 units, while Cleaning Supplies recorded the lowest with 55,887 units.

5. Bakery had the highest sale value rate at 51.6%, while Snacks had the lowest at 48.0%.

6. Snacks recorded the highest number of out-of-stock reorders (126), indicating frequent stock shortages.

7. A total of 70,888 units require urgent restocking, with Popcorn having the highest requirement of 2,532 units.

8. Only 33.4% of deliveries were on time, which may contribute to stock shortages.

9. Meat holds the highest inventory value at 6.37M and requires careful stock management to reduce potential waste.


## Recommendations

- Prioritize restocking the Top 10 urgent products, including Popcorn, Soda, and Granola Bars.
- Review purchasing quantities for slow-moving categories like Snacks.
- Monitor Meat inventory closely to reduce excess stock and potential waste.
- Work with suppliers to improve on-time delivery performance.
- Review monthly sales and stock reports regularly to make better purchasing decisions.


## Conclusion

The report shows that the business has a large amount of inventory, but stock is not moving efficiently. At the same time, several products require urgent restocking.

By monitoring sales trends, controlling excess inventory, prioritizing urgent refills, and improving supplier delivery performance, the business can **reduce blocked capital, avoid stock shortages, and improve inventory management**.
